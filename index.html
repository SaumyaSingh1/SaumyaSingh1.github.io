SKU-01 · OCT 2026 – PRESENT Flagship

AI Brand Health & Sentiment Engine

Consumer insights × GenAI · Python, Gemini, Power BI

1,140 messy reviews in. 1,913 structured insights out.

  • Built an end-to-end LLM pipeline (Python, Gemini 3.5 Flash, pydantic schemas) that worked within a 20-request/day API limit using batching, auto-save and resume.
  • Validated the output against 43 blind hand-labelled reviews (95% sentiment agreement). The model under-flags churn, so I reported churn as a conservative lower bound.
  • Shipped a Power BI dashboard (competitive matrix, root-cause decomposition tree, KPI cards, 8 DAX measures) that drills from 1,075 complaints to the exact product defect.
  • Turned the findings into a 7-slide repositioning deck with brand-specific interventions and a measurable test plan.

SKU-03 · AUG 2026 GenAI

BNS AI Legal Assistant

RAG chatbot on India's new criminal code · Python, ChromaDB, Gemini

358 sections of law. Every answer cites its source.

  • Parsed the official Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 Gazette PDF and rebuilt it as clean chapters and sections, stripping headers and bilingual noise.
  • Chunked by legal unit, not word count: 381 chunks tagged with Act, Chapter and Section, embedded locally in ChromaDB.
  • Retrieves the top matching provisions and grounds Gemini in them, so it must cite the exact section and says "not in BNS" instead of guessing.
  • Shipped as a Flask web chat app with a built-in legal disclaimer.
